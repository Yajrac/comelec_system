ABOUT THE PORTAL

Closer to the community.

Pio Duran Voter Registration helps applicants in Pio Duran, Albay begin their registration services online and prepare for the next steps at the registration office.

01

One place for your application

Start a new registration or request a correction, transfer, reactivation, or reinstatement of your record.

02

Prepare at your own pace

Complete your information and prepare supporting documents. New registration drafts can be saved in this browser and synced when online.

03

Guided by the registration office

Online submission is the first step. Applications remain subject to office review, and fingerprint and signature capture are completed during your office visit.
